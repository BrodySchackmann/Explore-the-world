About Kyrgyzstan

Kyrgyzstan is a landlocked country in Central Asia. It borders Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan and China. Its capital city is Bishkek. Much of the country is covered by the Tian Shan mountains, making it famous for its beautiful mountain landscapes and lakes.

5 Interesting Facts About Kyrgyzstan

  1. The capital city of Kyrgyzstan is Bishkek.
  2. The national language is Kyrgyz, and Russian is also widely used.
  3. Lake Issyk-Kul is one of the world's largest mountain lakes.
  4. Traditional Kyrgyz homes called yurts are portable round tents.
  5. The traditional sport Kok-Boru is played on horseback.
