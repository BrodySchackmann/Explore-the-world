🇲🇬 Madagascar

About Madagascar

Madagascar is a large island country in the Indian Ocean, off the southeastern coast of Africa. Its capital city is Antananarivo. Madagascar is famous for its unique wildlife, tropical forests and baobab trees.

5 Interesting Facts About Madagascar

  1. Antananarivo is the capital city of Madagascar.
  2. Madagascar is the fourth-largest island in the world.
  3. Lemurs naturally live in the wild in Madagascar.
  4. Malagasy and French are the country's official languages.
  5. Madagascar is famous for its giant baobab trees.
