About Uzbekistan

Uzbekistan is a landlocked country in Central Asia. It shares borders with Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Afghanistan and Turkmenistan. Its capital city is Tashkent. Uzbekistan is famous for its historic cities, colourful architecture and connection to the ancient Silk Road.

5 Interesting Facts About Uzbekistan

  1. The capital city of Uzbekistan is Tashkent.
  2. The official language is Uzbek.
  3. Samarkand is a historic city that was an important stop on the Silk Road.
  4. The Registan Square in Samarkand is famous for its beautiful Islamic architecture.
  5. Uzbekistan is famous for traditional foods such as plov, a rice dish often made with meat and vegetables.
