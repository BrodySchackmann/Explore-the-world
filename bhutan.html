About Bhutan

Bhutan is a small landlocked country in South Asia, located in the eastern Himalayas between India and China. Its capital city is Thimphu. The country is famous for its beautiful mountains, Buddhist monasteries and traditional culture.

5 Interesting Facts About Bhutan

  1. Bhutan is located in the Himalayan mountains.
  2. The capital city of Bhutan is Thimphu.
  3. The national language is Dzongkha.
  4. Bhutan is famous for its Tiger's Nest Monastery, built on a cliff.
  5. Bhutan measures progress using Gross National Happiness.
