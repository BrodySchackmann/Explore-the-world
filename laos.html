About Laos

Laos is a landlocked country in Southeast Asia. It is surrounded by China, Vietnam, Cambodia, Thailand and Myanmar. Its capital city is Vientiane. Laos is known for its mountains, rivers and Buddhist temples.

5 Interesting Facts About Laos

  1. Vientiane is the capital city of Laos.
  2. Laos is the only landlocked country in Southeast Asia.
  3. The Mekong River is one of its most important rivers.
  4. Luang Prabang is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
  5. The Plain of Jars contains thousands of ancient stone jars.
