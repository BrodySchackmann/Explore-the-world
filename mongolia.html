About Mongolia

Mongolia is a landlocked country in East Asia, located between Russia and China. Its capital city is Ulaanbaatar. The country is known for its large grasslands, the Gobi Desert, mountains and traditional nomadic lifestyle.

5 Interesting Facts About Mongolia

  1. The capital city of Mongolia is Ulaanbaatar.
  2. The official language is Mongolian.
  3. Mongolia is famous for its nomadic traditions and traditional round homes called gers.
  4. The Gobi Desert, one of Asia's largest deserts, is partly located in Mongolia.
  5. The Naadam Festival celebrates traditional sports, including horse racing, wrestling and archery.
